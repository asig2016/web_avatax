AVATAX A.E. acts as **fiscal representative** for companies established in EU member states as well as in third countries (outside the EU), provided that these countries have a corresponding agreement with the EU.

## What we do as your fiscal representative

- obtain a Greek tax registration number (VAT registration) with the tax authorities on your behalf
- keep your VAT account and file the related returns
- keep the relevant invoices and documents in our offices
- act as your contact for the Greek tax authorities

We work in German and English, so communication with your head office abroad is straightforward.
