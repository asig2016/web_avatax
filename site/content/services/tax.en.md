Anyone who earns income in Greece, owns property there or moves their residence to Greece deals with a separate tax system, its own forms and its own deadlines. We advise foreign companies and individuals in English and German.

## Companies

- ongoing tax advice for Greek subsidiaries and branches: corporate income tax, VAT, withholding taxes
- tax returns and support during tax audits
- Greek tax number and tax representation of foreign companies ([tax & fiscal representation](/services/fiscal-representation/))

## Individuals

- **Income tax:** for residents and non-residents of Greece, including rental income from Greek property
- **Foreign pensions:** allocation of taxing rights under the applicable double tax treaty
- **Real estate:** transfer tax on purchase (usually 3% plus a municipal surcharge), the annual property tax ENFIA and the real estate declaration (E9)
- **Inheritances and gifts:** involving Greece; Greek law applies tax classes according to the degree of kinship
- **Greek tax number (AFM):** required for property purchases, leases and bank accounts

## Moving to Greece

Special tax regimes apply to pensioners ([7% flat tax, Art. 5B](/services/relocating-to-greece/pensioners/)) and to employees and freelancers ([50% exemption, Art. 5C](/services/relocating-to-greece/employees-and-freelancers/)). Investors can opt for a [€100,000 flat tax on foreign income (Art. 5A)](/services/relocating-to-greece/flat-tax-for-investors/).
