Anyone who earns income in Greece, owns property there or moves their residence to Greece deals with a separate tax system, its own forms and its own deadlines. We advise foreign companies and individuals in English and German and explain the Greek rules in comparison with those of the other country concerned.

Looking for a tax adviser in Greece? Greece has no separate tax adviser profession of the kind found in some other countries, such as the German Steuerberater. Tax returns, tax advice and representation before the tax administration are mainly handled by accountants licensed by the Economic Chamber of Greece as Λογιστής-Φοροτεχνικός (accountant and tax practitioner) – as at our firm.

- tax returns and support during tax audits
- Greek tax number and tax representation of foreign companies ([tax & fiscal representation](/services/fiscal-representation/))

## Individuals

- **Income tax:** for residents and non-residents of Greece, including rental income from Greek property
- **Foreign pensions:** allocation of taxing rights under the double tax treaty between Greece and the paying country, depending on the type of pension – under the 1966 treaty with Germany, for example, statutory pensions are generally taxed in Greece and civil-service pensions in Germany; company and private pensions follow their own rules. The [7% flat tax under Art. 5B](/services/relocating-to-greece/pensioners/) may also apply
- **Real estate:** transfer tax on purchase (usually 3% plus a municipal surcharge), the annual property tax ENFIA, the real estate declaration (E9) and the taxation of rental income – more on [buying property](/services/start-in-greece/private-individuals/buying-property/) and [renting out property](/services/start-in-greece/private-individuals/renting-out-property/)
- **Inheritances and gifts:** involving Greece; Greek law applies tax classes according to the degree of kinship. We coordinate any interaction with inheritance tax in another country with your tax adviser there
- **Greek tax number (AFM):** required for property purchases, leases and bank accounts; we apply for it on your behalf

## Moving to Greece

Investors can opt for a [€100,000 flat tax on foreign income (Art. 5A)](/services/relocating-to-greece/flat-tax-for-investors/). Special tax regimes also apply to pensioners ([7% flat tax, Art. 5B](/services/relocating-to-greece/pensioners/)) and to employees and freelancers ([50% exemption, Art. 5C](/services/relocating-to-greece/employees-and-freelancers/)).
