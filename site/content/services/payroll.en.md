Do you want to employ staff in Greece – with or without your own Greek company? Greek labour and social security law differs in many respects from that of other countries: 14 salaries a year, registrations before work starts, electronic recording of working time in real time. We take care of all formalities and the ongoing payroll and communicate with your HR department in English or German.

For employers from Germany we set out the Greek rules against the German ones – for example on salary payments, annual leave, termination and social security.

## Employing staff in Greece without a Greek company

A foreign company without a permanent establishment in Greece may employ people resident in Greece – provided their activities do not create a permanent establishment under the applicable double tax treaty or Greek tax law. Activities such as IT, accounting, quality assurance or consulting usually do not create a permanent establishment, whereas sales activities and concluding contracts on behalf of the company may; we assess this before the hiring. Other ways of operating without a Greek company: [doing business in Greece without an establishment](/services/start-in-greece/companies/without-an-establishment/).

We take care of all steps:

- **employment contract** in line with Greek labour law, in Greek or bilingual and signed in original – based on your template or ours. Compared with contracts used in other countries, the 14 salary payments a year (including the Christmas, Easter and holiday bonuses), annual leave and termination under Greek law usually have to be set out explicitly
- **registrations:** registration as an employer with e-EFKA (social security) and declaration of the hiring in ERGANI II before employment starts
- **Greek tax number** for your company, so that payroll tax can be paid – with the appointment of a **tax representative** or, alternatively, registration of your managing director in the Greek tax register
- **monthly payroll**, social security declarations and one consolidated payroll tax return for all employees in Greece; payments can be made through our client account

## Working with your HR department

We agree a clear monthly process:

1. By an agreed cut-off date (e.g. the 20th of the month) we receive leave, sickness and other data and prepare the payroll.
2. Payment is made either in full through our client account, or as net salary directly to the employee with taxes and contributions paid through us.
3. Later changes are confirmed in the first working days of the following month and settled with the next payment.
4. By the end of the following month, payroll tax and social security contributions are declared and paid.

## Payroll for your Greek company or branch

- monthly payroll including the Christmas, Easter and holiday bonuses
- hirings, departures and changes in working hours, including the declarations in **ERGANI II**
- social security contributions to **e-EFKA** and payroll tax to the tax administration
- annual payroll certificates

We answer your specific questions – for example on permitted arrangements in employment contracts (probation, part-time work, telework), flexible working-time arrangements, social security obligations, annual leave and the termination of employment. In complex cases we work together with lawyers.
