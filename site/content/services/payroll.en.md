Payroll in Greece involves numerous electronic filings and strict deadlines. We take care of the complete payroll administration for your company.

## Our payroll services

- monthly payroll calculation and payslips
- hirings, changes and departures of employees, including the related notifications (ERGANI)
- social security contributions and the related filings (e-EFKA)
- payroll tax withholding and the related returns
- advice on day-to-day labour law questions in cooperation with your legal advisers
