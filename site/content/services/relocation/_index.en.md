If you move to Greece, you may be able to use one of three special tax regimes. Which one applies depends on whether you come as an investor, move as a pensioner or work in Greece. We check whether you qualify, put together the documents, file the application and then take care of your annual tax returns – in English or German. For clients from Germany we also take the German side into account – for example pension notices, certificates of residence and the Germany–Greece double tax treaty.

## Which regime applies to you?

- **Investors:** all foreign-source income is covered by a flat tax of €100,000 a year for up to 15 years, if you invest at least €500,000 in Greece (Art. 5A of the Greek Income Tax Code).
- **Pensioners:** all foreign-source income is taxed at a flat rate of 7% for 15 years (Art. 5B).
- **Employees and freelancers:** 50% of the income from work in Greece is exempt from income tax for seven years (Art. 5C).
