Greece encourages people to move there with three special tax regimes. Which one applies depends on whether you move as a pensioner, to work in Greece or as an investor. We check whether you qualify, put together the documents, file the application and then take care of your annual tax returns – in English or German.

## Which regime applies to you?

- **Pensioners:** all foreign-source income is taxed at a flat rate of 7% for 15 years (Art. 5B of the Greek Income Tax Code).
- **Employees and freelancers:** 50% of the income from work in Greece is exempt from income tax for seven years (Art. 5C).
- **Investors:** all foreign-source income is covered by a flat tax of €100,000 a year for up to 15 years, if you invest at least €500,000 in Greece (Art. 5A).
