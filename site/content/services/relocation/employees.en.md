Employees and freelancers who move their tax residence to Greece can have **50% of their income from work in Greece exempted from income tax for seven years** (Art. 5C of the Greek Income Tax Code). We check whether you qualify, put together the documents, file the application and then take care of your annual tax returns – in English or German.

## Conditions

- you were not a Greek tax resident in five of the last six years,
- you move your tax residence from an EU member state or a country with an agreement on administrative cooperation in tax matters,
- you are employed by a Greek company or the Greek permanent establishment of a foreign company, or you carry on a self-employed activity in Greece, and
- you declare that you will remain tax resident in Greece for at least two years.

## Taxation

- **50% of the income** from employment or self-employment in Greece is exempt from income tax **for seven years**.

## Typical documents

- proof of previous tax residence abroad (as for [Art. 5B](/services/relocating-to-greece/pensioners/))
- a declaration that you will remain tax resident in Greece for at least two years
- for employees: the employment contract and a declaration by the employer that it is a new position; for directors and board members, the publication of the appointment in the business register (GEMI)
- for freelancers: proof of the registration of the activity with the tax office

## Formalities for foreign documents

Foreign documents usually have to be notarised and bear an apostille. Exceptions apply, among others, to applications under a double tax treaty and to certificates of tax residence from states that are party to the Convention abolishing the requirement of legalisation for foreign public documents. Copies are accepted if a Greek lawyer or notary certifies them as true copies.

## How we support you

1. **Assessment** of your eligibility and the current application deadlines
2. **Documents:** a list of what is needed and support with certifications, apostilles and translations
3. **Application** to the Greek tax administration (AADE), including the Greek tax number
4. **Ongoing support:** annual tax returns with the exemption applied
