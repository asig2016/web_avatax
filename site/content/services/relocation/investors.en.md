Individuals who transfer their tax residence to Greece and invest there can pay a **flat tax of €100,000 a year on all their foreign-source income**, regardless of its amount, for up to 15 years (Art. 5A of the Greek Income Tax Code, the Greek "non-dom" regime). We check whether you qualify, coordinate the application with the investment and then take care of your annual tax returns – in English or German.

## Conditions

- you were not a Greek tax resident in seven of the last eight years before the move,
- you transfer your tax residence to Greece, and
- you invest at least €500,000 in Greece – in real estate, businesses, securities or shares in companies based in Greece – within three years of the application.

## Taxation

- All foreign-source income is covered by a **flat tax of €100,000 per tax year**, regardless of its amount.
- The regime applies for up to **15 tax years**, starting with the year for which you apply.
- The tax is paid in one instalment by the last working day of December; it cannot be offset against other tax liabilities or credits.
- Tax paid abroad on the same income is not credited.
- Greek-source income is taxed under the general rules.
- Movable property abroad is exempt from Greek inheritance and gift tax.

## Family members

Your spouse or partner and relatives such as children and parents can be included, either with your application or later during the 15 years. The flat tax is **€20,000 per year for each family member** included.

## Investment

The investment of at least €500,000 must be completed within three years of the application. If it is not completed, the regime is withdrawn. Which investments count and how they are documented should be clarified before the application.

## Application

The application is filed with the Greek tax administration (AADE) – online via myAADE, by post or in person. The previous fixed deadline of 31 March has been abolished; the current filing deadlines depend on the year of the move, so we check the deadline that applies to you.

## Typical documents

- proof of previous tax residence abroad (as for the [7% regime](/services/relocating-to-greece/pensioners/))
- documents on the investment in Greece
- for family members: documents proving the relationship

## Formalities for foreign documents

Foreign documents usually have to be notarised and bear an apostille. Exceptions apply, among others, to applications under a double tax treaty and to certificates of tax residence from states that are party to the Convention abolishing the requirement of legalisation for foreign public documents. Copies are accepted if a Greek lawyer or notary certifies them as true copies.
