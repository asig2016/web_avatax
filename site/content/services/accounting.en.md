We keep our clients' accounting records entirely in our offices, using modern accounting software, and give you **online access** so that you can retrieve information about your company at any time. You are relieved of complex administrative work and can concentrate fully on your business.

## Our accounting services

- bookkeeping in accordance with Greek accounting standards, including the electronic books (myDATA)
- annual financial statements, prepared and signed by our licensed accountants (class A)
- interim reports – monthly, quarterly or on request (reporting, cash flow etc.)
- support during tax audits
- **preventive tax reviews** of the books, so that omissions or irregularities are corrected in good time and you are well prepared for any future tax audit

Would you rather keep your own accounting department? We also [organise and support accounting departments](/services/accounting-department-support/).
