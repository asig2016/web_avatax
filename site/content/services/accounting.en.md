Greek [subsidiaries](/services/start-in-greece/companies/subsidiary/), [branches](/services/start-in-greece/companies/branch/) and sole proprietorships keep their books under Greek law and transmit their data electronically to the tax administration on an ongoing basis. We take care of the complete accounting – on request including daily bank reconciliation and payments – and report to you or your parent company in English or German.

## Key features of Greek accounting

- **Accounting standards:** the basis are the Greek accounting standards (Law 4308/2014). IFRS are mandatory only in certain cases, for example for listed companies and for companies included in IFRS consolidated financial statements that reach defined thresholds (5% of consolidated turnover, total assets or results). IFRS may be applied voluntarily.
- **Electronic books (myDATA):** invoices and their accounting classification are transmitted continuously to the tax administration's myDATA platform.
- **E-invoicing:** between Greek businesses, electronic invoices have been mandatory since February 2026 for companies with revenue above EUR 1 million; all other businesses follow in the course of 2026.
- **Publication:** companies publish their financial statements in the Greek business register (GEMI).
- **Income tax:** the corporate income tax rate is 22%; for calendar-year companies the return is due by 30 June of the following year.
