Foreign companies have four basic ways to do business in Greece; a fifth case is the mere acquisition of property in Greece. They differ in legal, tax and accounting terms – and in cost, liability and how you appear in the market. Together with you, we work out the appropriate way, explain the tax, labour-law and company-law consequences, support the set-up and take over your accounting and tax obligations from the first business day. On legal matters we work together with your legal advisers.

## Which way suits you?

- **[Without an establishment](/services/start-in-greece/companies/without-an-establishment/):** you sell or provide services from abroad, send your own staff or employ individual staff in Greece – often the first step.
- **[Representative office](/services/start-in-greece/companies/representative-office/):** a contact and coordination office without business of its own, usually not taxable.
- **[Branch](/services/start-in-greece/companies/branch/):** a registered permanent establishment of your company with its own business activity, taxable in Greece.
- **[Subsidiary](/services/start-in-greece/companies/subsidiary/):** an independent Greek company, usually an I.K.E. or A.E.
- **[Property acquisition](/services/start-in-greece/companies/property-acquisition/):** buying property in Greece through your company – without a branch or subsidiary, requiring only a registration with the Greek tax authority (Greek tax number).

After the start, we take care of your [accounting](/services/accounting/), [payroll](/services/payroll/) and [tax matters](/services/tax-consultancy/) and act as your [tax or fiscal representative](/services/fiscal-representation/).
