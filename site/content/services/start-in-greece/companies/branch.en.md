A branch (υποκατάστημα) is not legally independent but part of the foreign company; it does, however, carry on business activities of its own. It is registered in the Greek business register (GEMI), constitutes a permanent establishment and is subject to income tax in Greece. A key issue is the allocation of profits between the branch and the head office. Its staff are legally employed by the head office but are economically attributable to the branch. For a specific project, a project branch can be registered under a simplified procedure.

## Accounting and tax

- The branch keeps books in Greece under Greek accounting rules and files its own Greek tax returns.
- Profits are allocated between branch and head office according to the functions and risks of each; this allocation should be documented.
- Staff working for the branch are registered in Greece for payroll and social security.
