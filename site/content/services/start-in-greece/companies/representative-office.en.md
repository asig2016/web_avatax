A representative office has no legal personality of its own and merely represents the interests of the foreign company. It does not conclude business of its own but is limited to contact and coordination tasks. As long as it carries out only such preparatory or auxiliary activities, it normally does not constitute a permanent establishment and is therefore not subject to Greek income tax. Its staff are attributable to the head office both legally and economically.

## Typical tasks

- market research and contacts with customers, suppliers and authorities
- coordination on behalf of the head office
- support for the head office's sales, without concluding contracts of its own

## What to watch out for

As soon as the office negotiates or concludes contracts on behalf of the company, or carries out business activities of its own, it can become a permanent establishment – with income tax and accounting obligations in Greece. The tasks should therefore be clearly defined from the start.

## How we support you

1. **Assessment** of the planned tasks and the permanent establishment risk
2. **Registration** with the tax administration and, where required, the employer registration
3. **Ongoing support:** payroll for the staff, the remaining filings in Greece and – for companies without a Greek company – tax representation, in English or German
