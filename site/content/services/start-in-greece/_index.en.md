A start in Greece can mean very different things: a foreign company that wants to sell, set up an office or found a subsidiary – or a private individual who buys a holiday home, spends several months a year in Greece or moves there to work self-employed. Each case has its own tax and accounting consequences. We explain them before you decide and take care of the obligations afterwards – in English or German.

If you plan to move your tax residence to Greece, see also the [tax incentives for moving to Greece](/services/relocating-to-greece/).
