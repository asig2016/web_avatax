Whether your company starts doing business in Greece, you acquire property – including to [rent it out](/services/start-in-greece/private-individuals/renting-out-property/) – or you move your residence to Greece: each plan has consequences for taxation, accounting and social security. We explain them before you decide and then take care of accounting, payroll and tax returns in Greece – in English or German.

Special tax regimes apply to people moving to Greece: a €100,000 flat tax for investors, a 7% flat tax for pensioners and a 50% income tax exemption for employees and freelancers. More under [tax incentives for moving to Greece](/services/relocating-to-greece/).
