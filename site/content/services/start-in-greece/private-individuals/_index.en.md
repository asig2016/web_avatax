Private individuals start in Greece in different ways: they buy a holiday home or an apartment to [rent out](/services/start-in-greece/private-individuals/renting-out-property/), spend several months a year in Greece on a rental, or move there to work self-employed. From the Greek tax number to the annual property tax, social insurance and the tax return, we take care of the tax side.

Moving your tax residence to Greece? Special regimes apply to [investors](/services/relocating-to-greece/flat-tax-for-investors/), [pensioners](/services/relocating-to-greece/pensioners/), and [employees and freelancers](/services/relocating-to-greece/employees-and-freelancers/): [tax incentives for moving to Greece](/services/relocating-to-greece/).
