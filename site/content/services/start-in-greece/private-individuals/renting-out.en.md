If you rent out a property in Greece, the rental income is taxed in Greece, wherever you live. It is not added to your other income but taxed on a scale of its own. We register the rental, prepare the annual tax return and coordinate with your tax adviser at home.

## How rental income is taxed

Rental income of individuals is taxed separately, without a tax-free allowance. For income earned from 2026:

| Annual rental income | Tax rate |
|---|---|
| up to €12,000 | 15% |
| €12,001 to €24,000 | 25% |
| €24,001 to €36,000 | 35% |
| over €36,000 | 45% |

- **Costs:** Instead of actual expenses, a flat deduction of 5% of the rent is granted for repairs and maintenance. Depreciation and loan interest are not deductible.
- **Payment through a bank:** The rent has to be paid into a bank account reported to the tax administration; otherwise the flat deduction is lost.
- **ENFIA:** The owner also pays the annual property tax.

## The owner's duties

- **Tax number (AFM):** required for every step; we apply for it on your behalf.
- **Registering the lease:** The owner reports each lease electronically to the tax administration (AADE) and the tenant confirms it.
- **Annual tax return:** Rental income is declared in the Greek income tax return with the schedule for property (E2).

## Short-term rental

Short-term rental means letting a furnished property for less than 60 days without services other than bed linen, for example through booking platforms.

- **Register:** The property is entered in AADE's short-term rental register; the register number (ΑΜΑ) must appear in every listing.
- **Up to two properties:** The income counts as rental income and is taxed on the scale above.
- **Three or more properties:** The rental counts as a business activity, with business registration, bookkeeping and VAT.
- **Levies and local restrictions:** A levy is due per night, and new registrations are restricted in some areas. We check the rules currently in force.

## Double taxation

Double taxation treaties generally give the right to tax income from immovable property to the country where the property is located. Your home country then either exempts the income or credits the Greek tax against its own. Which method applies, and whether the income affects your tax rate at home, depends on the treaty with your country. We provide the Greek figures and tax certificates your adviser at home needs.

## What changes with a company

If a company owns and rents out the property, different rules apply:

- **Tax rate:** The company pays corporate income tax at a single rate of 22% on its profit; distributions to the shareholders bear a further 5% withholding tax.
- **Costs:** Actual expenses and depreciation are deductible; the flat deduction does not apply.
- **Bookkeeping:** The company keeps double-entry books and prepares annual financial statements.
- **Short-term rental:** For a company it always counts as a business activity with VAT.
- **Transfer:** Moving a property you already own privately into the company triggers transfer tax.
- **Foreign company:** The annual special real estate tax also has to be considered, see [property holding company](/services/start-in-greece/companies/property-holding-company/).

Which form costs less depends on the individual case. We calculate both options before you decide.
