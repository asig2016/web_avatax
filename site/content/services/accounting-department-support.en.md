The accounting department is a key part of every company. Well organised, it not only prevents errors and irregularities but also provides management with reliable information on sales and costs – a real tool for decision-making.

## How we support you

- **set-up:** our experienced staff organise the accounting departments of new companies and design their structure in close cooperation with you
- **supervision and support:** for companies whose staff handle day-to-day accounting without specialised knowledge, we supervise the work, answer questions directly and so reduce the workload and the risk of errors
- **senior accountants:** our licensed accountants (class A) prepare and sign the financial statements, support you during tax audits and prepare interim reports on a monthly or quarterly basis
- **preventive tax reviews** of your books, so that you are well prepared for any future tax audit
