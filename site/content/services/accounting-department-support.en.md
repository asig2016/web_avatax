The accounting department is a key part of every company. Well organised, it not only prevents errors and irregularities but also provides management with reliable information on sales and costs – a real tool for decision-making.

## How we support you

- **Set-up:** our experienced staff organise the accounting departments of new companies and design their structure in close cooperation with you
- **Supervision and support:** for companies whose staff handle day-to-day accounting without specialised knowledge, we supervise the work, review the entries, answer questions directly and so reduce the workload and the risk of errors; your staff continue the day-to-day bookkeeping
- **Licensed accountants:** our licensed accountants (class A) prepare and sign the financial statements, support you during tax audits and prepare interim reports on a monthly or quarterly basis
- **Preventive tax reviews:** we review your books so that you are well prepared for any future tax audit

## Who it is for

- **new companies** that want to build their own accounting department from the start
- **established companies** whose staff handle day-to-day accounting without specialised knowledge
- **Greek subsidiaries of foreign groups** that keep their own accounting staff but need support with Greek accounting and tax rules; we report in English or German if required
