Greece encourages people to move there with two special tax regimes: a **7% flat tax for pensioners** (Art. 5B of the Greek Income Tax Code) and a **50% income tax exemption for employees and freelancers** (Art. 5C). We check whether you qualify, put together the documents, file the application and then take care of your annual tax returns – in English or German.

## Pensioners: 7% flat tax (Art. 5B)

### Conditions

- you transfer your tax residence to Greece as a pensioner,
- you were not a Greek tax resident in five of the last six years, and
- you move from an EU member state or a country with which Greece has an agreement on administrative cooperation in tax matters.

### Taxation

- All foreign-source income is taxed at a **flat rate of 7% for 15 years**.
- The tax is paid in one instalment by the last working day of July; it cannot be offset against other tax liabilities or credits.
- Greek-source income is taxed under the general rules.
- Double tax treaties remain unaffected; under certain conditions, tax withheld abroad can be credited.
- The regime does not apply to gift and inheritance tax.
- Each person applies separately; spouses are not included.

### Typical documents

- proof of pensioner status, e.g. the latest pension notice or a certificate from the pension provider (with apostille)
- proof of previous tax residence abroad, e.g. a certificate of tax residence or – for countries with a double tax treaty – the treaty application form confirmed by the foreign tax office
- documents on residence in Greece, e.g. lease or purchase contract, form S1 from the pension provider, EU registration certificate and Greek social security number (AMKA)

Whether you qualify as a pensioner under the regime should be checked in advance – certain types of pension, such as some accident pensions from private insurance, are excluded.

## Employees and freelancers: 50% exemption (Art. 5C)

### Conditions

- you were not a Greek tax resident in five of the last six years,
- you move your tax residence from an EU member state or a country with an agreement on administrative cooperation in tax matters,
- you are employed by a Greek company or the Greek permanent establishment of a foreign company, or you carry on a self-employed activity in Greece, and
- you declare that you will remain tax resident in Greece for at least two years.

### Taxation

- **50% of the income** from employment or self-employment in Greece is exempt from income tax **for seven years**.

### Typical documents

- proof of previous tax residence abroad (as for Art. 5B)
- a declaration that you will remain tax resident in Greece for at least two years
- for employees: the employment contract and a declaration by the employer that it is a new position; for directors and board members, the publication of the appointment in the business register (GEMI)
- for freelancers: proof of the registration of the activity with the tax office

## Formalities for foreign documents

Foreign documents usually have to be notarised and bear an apostille. Exceptions apply, among others, to applications under a double tax treaty and to certificates of tax residence from states that are party to the Convention abolishing the requirement of legalisation for foreign public documents. Copies are accepted if a Greek lawyer or notary certifies them as true copies.
