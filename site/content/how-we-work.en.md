We look after every mandate personally and with fixed routines – so that you know who is responsible for you and when you receive what from us.

## Personal support

- **Dedicated contact person:** Every mandate is looked after by one contact person who knows your business or your situation and coordinates your matters with our senior staff.
- **Four-eyes review:** A second professional reviews financial statements, tax returns and answers before they leave our office.
- **Timely information:** We tell you early about foreseeable difficulties and upcoming deadlines.

## Communication

- **Languages:** We advise in English, German and Greek and explain what is different in Greece.
- **Channels:** by e-mail, phone or video; meetings are also possible in our office in Athens.
- **Reports to your parent company:** on request monthly or quarterly, in the format and chart of accounts of your parent company.

## Digital processes

- **Online access:** We keep your books in accounting software with online access; current figures are available to you at any time.
- **Electronic books:** We transmit invoices and their accounting classification to the tax authority's myDATA platform on an ongoing basis.
- **Fixed cut-off dates:** For payroll we agree a monthly routine with fixed dates by which you send us the data.
