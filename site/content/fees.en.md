## Clear. Based on the work. Transparent.

Depending on the service, we agree time-based fees or monthly flat fees. What counts is the agreed scope of services, the volume of work and the advice actually needed. Before every meeting, you receive a written offer setting out the scope of services and terms. The meeting takes place after you accept the offer in writing.

## Our fee models

- **Accounting and financial statements:** monthly flat fee, based on the scope of the ongoing bookkeeping, in particular the number of entries.
- **Payroll:** monthly flat fee, depending on the number of employees and the payroll workload.
- **Tax advice and individual assignments:** as a rule, time-based fees according to the actual work.
- **First meetings:** free of charge for companies; for private individuals a first consultation at a flat fee.

We tell you about foreseeable additional costs before they arise.

## Companies: free first meeting

In a free first meeting we clarify what you need and how a long-term cooperation could look – by video or in our office in Athens. For any ongoing support following this meeting, you receive a separate written offer.

## Private individuals: first consultation

Specific individual questions – for example on the taxation of a pension, a property or a move to Greece – are answered in a first consultation at a **fixed flat fee**.

- **Included:** a meeting by video or in our office in Athens, in which we discuss your question on the basis of your information and give you a first professional assessment.
- **Not included:** reviewing extensive documents, written opinions, tax returns and applications, and ongoing support; for these you receive a separate offer.

## Start of the mandate

Once you accept our offer in writing, you receive a written engagement confirmation with the scope of services and terms, the list of documents and powers of attorney we need, and your dedicated contact person. How we then look after your mandate: [How we work](/how-we-work/).
