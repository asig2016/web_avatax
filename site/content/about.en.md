## Our principles

Reliability is our guiding principle. Out of respect for our clients, we inform them in good time about any difficulties that may arise during our work or about additional fees. We build relationships of trust and are there for our clients whenever they need us.

## Our people

Our team combines university graduates with experienced professionals in accounting and taxation. All staff receive training on recruitment and regularly attend seminars, so that they are always up to date with current developments in accounting and tax.

Our staff's very good knowledge of English and German is a great advantage: it makes communication with authorities and companies abroad – especially in German-speaking countries – straightforward.

## Our offices

Our offices are on Syngrou Avenue, close to the metro station Syngrou-Fix: fully renovated premises with dedicated meeting rooms, easily accessible by public transport.
