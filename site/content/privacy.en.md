## Controller

AVATAX A.E., 76 Syngrou Ave., GR-117 42 Athens, Greece, phone +30 211 1036100.

## Visiting this website

When you visit this website, the web server records technical data (IP address, date and time, requested page, browser type) in log files. This is necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are deleted after at most 30 days. The website is hosted on a server in the EU (Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany).

## Contact form and applications

If you contact us via the contact form or send us an application, we process the data you enter (name, e-mail address, phone number, message and, for applications, your CV and the details you provide) solely to handle your request or application (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data is transmitted to us by e-mail and is not stored on the web server.

We delete enquiries once they have been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Applications are deleted no later than six months after the end of the application process, unless you consent to longer storage.

## Live chat

The chat button at the bottom of the page is provided via our own chat server (Rocket.Chat). The button is only shown while a member of our team is available; this is checked by our own web server, without transmitting any data about you to the chat server. Nothing is loaded from the chat server until you click "Start chat". When you start a chat, we process your messages, the name and e-mail address you enter, your IP address, the pages of this website you visit during the chat and the website and language from which you contact us, in order to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The chat stores an identifier in your browser's local storage so that the conversation continues on other pages. Chat transcripts are deleted once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Please do not send confidential documents or tax identification numbers via the chat.

## Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies by default. Only if you give your consent via the cookie banner do we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) to understand how the website is used (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). IP addresses are anonymised. You can withdraw your consent at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the page.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw any consent given with effect for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).
